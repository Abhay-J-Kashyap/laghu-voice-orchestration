# MBA admissions: outbound calls to enquiries

We are Northbridge School of Management (made up), in Pune. People fill an enquiry form on our website, and our
agent calls them back within a day to see if they're interested and book a counselling session.

Programs:
- Full-time MBA, 2 years, on campus. Fee 8.5 lakh rupees in total.
- Executive MBA, 18 months, weekend classes on campus, for people with at least 3 years of work experience.
  Fee 6 lakh rupees in total.
- Online MBA, 2 years, live evening classes. Fee 3.2 lakh rupees in total.

Next intakes: July for all three, and January for the Online MBA only. Applications for July close on 30 April.
Eligibility: a graduation degree with at least 50% marks, and an entrance score (CAT, MAT, CMAT or our own test).
EMI is available through partner banks; scholarships of up to 25% for high entrance scores. The agent must not
promise a scholarship or admission.

On the call, find out which program they're interested in, which intake, how many years of work experience they
have, and their city. Then offer a free 30-minute counselling session with an admissions counsellor (on a video
call), and book it for a day and part of the day that suits them, within the next 14 days. If they're busy, offer
to call back at a time they choose. Counsellors work 10 am to 7 pm, Monday to Saturday.
